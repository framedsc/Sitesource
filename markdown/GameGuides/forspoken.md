## Tools

* [Camera tools by ghostinthecamera](https://patreon.com/ghostinthecamera)  
**Dynamic light spawning** (Advanced tools): point and spot lights with full colour, intensity, range and shadow control, plus a scene light viewer to switch the game's own lights and shadows on or off for relighting  
**[Features](https://ghostinthecamera.co.uk/tools/forspoken/index.html)**: Typical camera tools features (camera, hotsampling, HUD etc), time control (native pause also pauses cutscene audio), shadow resolution, blur and FOV uncoupling, foliage, LOD and WorldLOD distance, post-process toggles, photo mode range removal
